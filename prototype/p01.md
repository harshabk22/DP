## 1. Executive Summary & Product Philosophy

Traditional dietary trackers fail because of two opposing design errors:
1. **The Fatigue Trap (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer):** Requiring manual text searches across bloated, uncurated databases and mandatory kitchen scale weighing. Studies show **80% user churn within 30 days**; users spending >15 min/day logging are 2.1× more likely to abandon by month 3.
2. **The Fraudulent Precision Trap (Cal AI, generic AI wrappers):** Promising that a single 2D smartphone photo produces exact calorie numbers down to the single digit. This ignores unobservable 3D bowl depth, food packing density, and invisible cooking oils (9 kcal/g).

### The Human-First Reality
> **"Cal AI guesses. MyFitnessPal interrogates. MessMate assists."**

Consumers want an **intelligent, encouraging personal dietitian in their pocket**, not an academic proctor:
- **Zero-Friction Multimodal Logging:** Snap a plate of food, OR speak/type: *"Chai and 2 digestive biscuits"*, *"Double egg omelet with brown bread"*.
- **Guidance Before Eating:** Users can ask the assistant: *"How much protein do I have left today?"*, *"What should I order at dinner to stay under 500 kcal?"*
- **Quiet Habit Learning:** The app remembers your usual breakfast mug, lunch tiffin, and regular portion defaults. It pre-populates these quantities without interrupting you with pop quizzes.
- **Zero-Faking Science:** Calorie estimates are expressed as realistic honest ranges (e.g., `620 ± 40 kcal [580 – 660]`), grounded 100% in certified USDA and Indian Food Composition (INDB/IFCT) tables (Invariant **I1**).

### A. Multimodal Quick-Logging (Voice / Text / Photo)
- **Speech & Natural Text:** Users tap a persistent mic button or type a quick snippet: *"Had 1 cup curd and an apple"*. The backend parses food items, standard units, and matches them to certified database rows in $<1.0\text{s}$.
- **Snap & Eat Photo Capture:** 1-tap camera capture with on-device hardware downsampling (800x800 WebP $<120\text{KB}$). Returns instant visual dish tags within 1.0s so the user can pocket their phone and eat hot food.

### B. In-App Conversational Nutrition Co-Pilot
- Accessible via a floating assistant sheet or dedicated tab.
- Answers user questions grounded strictly in their actual daily intake log and verified nutritional tables:
  - *"Can I fit an ice cream tonight?"* $\rightarrow$ *"You have 340 kcal remaining today. A standard single scoop (vanilla ~140 kcal) fits comfortably without exceeding your budget."*
  - Zero generative nutritional hallucinations: all macro totals come directly from the PostgreSQL store.

### C. Proactive 19:00 Dinner Gap Engine
- At 19:00, the system automatically compares daily logged nutrition against ICMR/USDA targets.
- Uses deterministic rule templates (0ms latency, \$0 cost):
  - Calculates remaining protein and fiber deficit.
  - Recommends the optimal dinner swap (e.g., *"Dinner menu has Rajma and Paneer; choosing Rajma covers your remaining 18g protein and 8g fiber deficit"*).

### D. Second Helpings: 1-Tap Timeline Refill Chips
- Users frequently grab second helpings without taking another photo.
- Meal cards on the timeline feature quick refill chips (`+1 Roti`, `+1 Katori Dal`, `+Half Cup Rice`).
- Tapping a refill updates meal totals instantly with zero pop-up questions.

### E. Contextual Memory & Habit Learning
- **Home Mode:** The app quietly remembers your preferred portion sizes and tableware defaults for your recurring dishes.
- **Dining Out / Restaurant Mode:** User portions are matched to standard commercial serving distributions without corrupting home tableware defaults.

## 3. Strict Non-Negotiable Invariants

| # | Invariant | Technical Enforcement & Guardrail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I1** | **LLM never outputs nutrient numbers** | The VLM prompt grammar strictly restricts output fields to dish labels, household units, unit counts, and estimated volume/grams. Calories, macros, and micronutrients are derived exclusively by joining against certified USDA/IFCT tables in PostgreSQL. |
| **I2** | **Distributions everywhere, never points** | Every quantity is tracked internally with mean and variance. The UI displays realistic honest ranges (e.g., `620 ± 40 kcal [580 – 660]`). |
| **I3** | **Zero interrogation spam** | The app defaults to intelligent habit estimates. Clarification is reserved for genuine high-uncertainty items, never exceeding 1 micro-interaction per meal. Refills never trigger questions. |
| **I4** | **Natural multimodal input** | Supports photo capture, natural voice transcripts, and text quick-logging. Zero friction for unphotographed snacks or drinks. |
| **I5** | **Clean auditability** | User corrections and edits update habit memory transparently while preserving raw meal history. |
| **I6** | **Transparent privacy** | Clear, standard consumer Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Full data export and account erasure (`DELETE /me`). |
| **I7** | **Under-18 registration block** | Registration checks `birth_year`. Users under 18 receive an immediate HTTP 422 reject. |
| **I8** | **BMR floor & zero shaming** | Calorie targets are clamped to $\max(\text{target}, \text{BMR})$ using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Shaming copy linter prohibits terms like *"over budget"*, *"failed"*, or *"burn off"*. |
| **I9** | **Zero questions answered must work completely** | Questions are strictly optional enhancements. A user who never answers a question still gets a complete, usable daily log. |
| **I10** | **Model & prompt versioning** | Version tags stored in `model_runs` alongside every inference for auditable debugging and thesis reproducibility. |

## 4. Mathematical & Analytical Engine

### 4.1 Exact Analytical Calorie & Variance Calculation (3 Lines of Math)
Instead of wasteful Monte Carlo stochastic loops ($N=2000$), we calculate total meal expectation and variance analytically in 2 microseconds:
$$\mathbb{E}[\text{Total}] = \sum_{i=1}^M c_i \cdot \text{grams\_mean}_i$$
$$\operatorname{Var}(\text{Total}) = \sum_{i=1}^M c_i^2 \cdot \text{grams\_sd}_i^2$$
$$\operatorname{SD}(\text{Total}) = \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\text{Total})}$$
- Honest UI Range: $[p_{10}, p_{90}] = [\mathbb{E} - 1.3 \cdot \operatorname{SD}, \mathbb{E} + 1.3 \cdot \operatorname{SD}]$, rounded to the nearest $10\text{ kcal}$.

### 4.2 Quiet Habit Learning (Exponential Moving Average)
When a user confirms or adjusts a portion at home:
$$\theta_{u, m, \text{new}} = (1 - \alpha) \theta_{u, m, \text{old}} + \alpha \cdot \frac{\text{mass}_{\text{observed}}}{n}$$
With learning rate $\alpha = 0.20$. The habit converges smoothly within 6–10 meals with zero matrix inversions or mathematical crashes.

## 6. Database Schema Design (PostgreSQL 15+)

### 6.1 Identity & Profiles
- `users`: `id (UUID PK)`, `email (citext UNIQUE)`, `hashed_password`, `created_at`.
- `profiles`: `user_id (FK users.id CASCADE)`, `birth_year (INT)`, `sex`, `height_cm`, `weight_kg`, `activity_level`, `goal`, `diet_type`.

### 6.2 Food Reference & Menus
- `foods`: `id (BIGSERIAL PK)`, `source (FNDDS | INDB | IFCT2017 | CURATED)`, `name_en (TEXT)`, `aliases (TEXT[])`, `kcal_per_100g`, `protein_g`, `fat_g`, `carb_g`, `fibre_g`, `density_g_per_ml`, `default_unit`.
  - **Index:** Trigram GIN index on `name_en` for $<5\text{ms}$ typo-tolerant fuzzy matching.
- `cafeteria_menus`: `id (BIGSERIAL PK)`, `cafeteria_id (TEXT)`, `day_of_week (0-6)`, `meal_slot`, `food_id (FK foods.id)`, `standard_serving_g`.

### 6.3 Meals, Items & Habit Memory
- `meals`: `id (UUID PK)`, `user_id (FK)`, `captured_at`, `meal_slot`, `source (photo | voice | text)`, `dining_context (home | away)`, `image_key`, `kcal_mean`, `kcal_sd`, `p10`, `p90`.
- `meal_items`: `id (UUID PK)`, `meal_id (FK meals.id CASCADE)`, `food_id (FK foods.id)`, `label`, `unit`, `unit_count`, `grams`, `kcal`, `is_refill (BOOL)`.
- `user_habits`: `user_id (FK)`, `unit (TEXT)`, `food_id (FK optional)`, `calibrated_grams`, `sample_count`, `updated_at`. `PRIMARY KEY (user_id, unit)`.
- `chat_messages`: `id (UUID PK)`, `user_id (FK)`, `role (user | assistant)`, `message (TEXT)`, `created_at`.
