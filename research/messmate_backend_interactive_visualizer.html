Stage 4 of 9 Algorithm: Log-Normal Conjugacy
Bayesian Tableware Volume Calibration
Instead of trusting a single noisy 2D optical photo, the backend uses conjugate Bayesian updating. Your plate's known volume serves as the prior distribution, and today's optical volume serves as the observation with a discretization noise shield.
Exact Formula Executed on CPU
σobs² = σoptical² + 1/(12n²)
μpost = σpost² × (μprior/σprior² + ln(Vobs)/σobs²)
1/σpost² = 1/σprior² + 1/σobs²
Why this matters (Spoon-Fed)
Every day you eat on the same plate, single-image optical noise cancels out. Over 10 meals, error approaches zero without you ever needing to place a credit card on the table or buy a LiDAR sensor.
